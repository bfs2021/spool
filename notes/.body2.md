### Problem statement

The control room has no place to paste a prompts.chat team URL; the hub half creates the loops and the channel, but only from the API. The web half: a "From prompts.chat team" action beside New loop that shows what will be created (loops, their roles, the channel, the handoffs) and creates it on confirm. Part of #1262.

### Coupling points

The Fleet page's New loop entry, channels in the control room, `web/src/api.ts` for the team route.

### Seams / ADRs touched

None new.

### Proposed shape

- Paste the URL, press Preview: a list of the loops to be created with their roles and one line of handoffs each, and the channel's name; Create does it and lands on the channel.
- A refusal shows the hub's reason inline.
- Fixture screenshots of the preview and the channel after creation.

### Open questions

None.

### Out of scope

Editing a member's mission before creation beyond what the draft allows (edit the loop after). Cloud loops: a team is created on bare or docker, the hub's default; a cloud loop is made one at a time with its own repo and environment (#1265).
